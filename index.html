Electric Insider Uploader

전기의 속사정 업로더 — a private publishing tool for one YouTube channel

What it is

This is an internal command-line tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel 전기의 속사정 (Electric Insider), an educational channel that explains the engineering behind everyday electrical and electronic devices. It is not a public service: there are no other users, no sign-up, and no website features beyond this page.

How it uses YouTube API Services

  • The channel owner signs in once with Google OAuth and grants the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes.
  • channels.list (mine=true) confirms the token belongs to the owner's channel before every upload.
  • videos.insert uploads a finished video produced by the owner and schedules its publication time.
  • thumbnails.set sets the custom thumbnail for that video.

The tool only acts on the owner's own channel. It does not read, store, or analyze any other user's data.
